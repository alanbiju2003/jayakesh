How to Use Jayakesh Kerala Hair Oil

  1. Part dry or slightly damp hair and dispense a small palmful of Jayakesh Kerala Hair Oil.
  2. Massage gently onto the scalp and hair roots using circular fingertip motions for 5–10 minutes.
  3. Smooth any remaining oil down the hair lengths and ends to condition strands.
  4. Leave on for at least 30–60 minutes (or overnight) before washing off with a mild cleanser. Apply 2–3 times weekly.
