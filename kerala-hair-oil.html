Why Traditional Handmade Kerala Hair Oil Stands Apart

Many mass-market commercial hair oils dilute plant extracts in light liquid paraffin (mineral oil), synthetic silicones, artificial dyes, and heavy perfumes that coat strands superficially without nourishing the scalp. Jayakesh takes the opposite route:

  • Pure Coconut Oil Base (Velichenna): Naturally rich in medium-chain fatty acids (especially lauric acid) with a high affinity for hair proteins, helping reduce protein loss in both undamaged and damaged hair.
  • Slow Small-Batch Preparation: Whole botanicals, bulbs, seeds, leaves, and blossoms are infused carefully by hand in Kerala so fat-soluble phytonutrients transfer gently into the oil.
  • ZERO Chemicals: Zero mineral oil, zero parabens, zero sulphates, zero synthetic fragrances, and zero artificial colours.
