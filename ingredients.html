The 9 Pure Natural Ingredients Inside Jayakesh

Not every ingredient is solely a herb — Jayakesh unites pure Coconut Oil, Red Onion, Hibiscus, Kalonji, Methi, Tulsi, Aloe Vera, Kali Mirch, and Tea Leaves with ZERO chemicals.

Every bottle of Jayakesh Kerala Hair Oil brings together nine time-tested natural ingredients — spanning nourishing carrier oil, sulphur-rich bulbs, conditioning blossoms, nutrient-dense seeds, soothing succulent gel, warming spice, and antioxidant leaves — crafted with ZERO chemicals.

1. Coconut Oil (Malayalam: Velichenna · Hindi: Nariyal Tel · Cocos nucifera)

The heart of authentic Kerala hair oiling. Pure coconut oil penetrates the hair shaft deeply thanks to its low molecular weight and lauric acid profile, helping reduce hair protein loss, prevent dryness, and soften coarse or frizzy strands.

2. Onion — Red Onion (Malayalam: Cheriya Ulli · Hindi: Lal Pyaz · Allium cepa)

Kerala red shallots and red onions are naturally rich in dietary sulphur compounds and quercetin antioxidants. Sulphur supports keratin — the structural protein of hair — helping nourish hair follicles, invigorate the scalp, and support strong, resilient strands.

3. Hibiscus (Malayalam: Chembarathi · Hindi: Gudhal · Tamil: Sembaruthi · Hibiscus rosa-sinensis)

A cornerstone of traditional Kerala thali hair care. Vibrant red hibiscus blossoms contain natural mucilage, amino acids, and anthocyanin antioxidants that condition hair, ease detangling, protect against oxidative stress, and promote natural lustre.

4. Kalonji — Black Seed (Malayalam: Karimjeerakam · Hindi: Kalonji · Nigella sativa)

Celebrated across traditional wellness systems, black seeds provide essential fatty acids (linoleic and oleic acids) alongside natural thymoquinone antioxidants that comfort the scalp and condition hair roots.

5. Methi — Fenugreek Seeds (Malayalam: Uluva · Hindi: Methi Dana · Tamil: Vendhayam · Trigonella foenum-graecum)

Golden fenugreek seeds contribute natural lecithin, plant proteins, nicotinic acid, and conditioning mucilage that smooth the hair cuticle, impart natural shine, and support root strength.

6. Tulsi — Holy Basil (Malayalam: Tulasi · Hindi: Tulsi · Ocimum tenuiflorum)

Fresh green tulsi sprigs with blossoms bring natural purifying compounds (eugenol and rosmarinic acid) and antioxidant protection to help keep the scalp clean, refreshed, and balanced.

7. Aloe Vera (Malayalam: Kattarvazha · Hindi: Ghritkumari · Aloe barbadensis)

Fresh aloe vera leaf gel provides soothing moisture, polysaccharides, and scalp comfort — helping hydrate dry scalps and leave hair soft, smooth, and manageable.

8. Kali Mirch — Black Pepper (Malayalam: Kurumulaku · Hindi: Kali Mirch · Tamil: Milagu · Piper nigrum)

Kerala's signature spice contains natural piperine and plant antioxidants, traditionally used in small measured amounts in Kerala oil infusions to gently warm and invigorate the scalp during massage.

9. Tea Leaves (Malayalam: Theyila · Hindi: Chai Patti · Camellia sinensis)

Rich in natural polyphenols, EGCG catechins, and caffeine, tea leaves help shield hair and scalp against everyday environmental oxidative stress while supporting healthy shine.
