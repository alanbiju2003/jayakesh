How to Use Jayakesh Kerala Hair Oil for Best Results

Master the traditional Kerala champi scalp massage routine — how much oil to apply, how long to leave it on, and how often to use Jayakesh for stronger, healthier-looking hair.

Consistent technique matters just as much as pure ingredients. Because Jayakesh Kerala Hair Oil is handmade with pure Coconut Oil, Red Onion, Hibiscus, Kalonji, Methi, Tulsi, Aloe Vera, Kali Mirch, and Tea Leaves with ZERO chemicals, a small amount goes a long way.

Step-by-Step Application Routine

  1. Detangle Gently First: Comb dry hair softly with a wide-tooth comb so loose shed hairs are cleared before massaging.
  2. Dispense 5–10 ml: Pour 1–2 teaspoons into your palms and rub your hands together briefly to warm the natural coconut base.
  3. Fingertip Scalp Massage (5–10 Minutes): Apply along partings from front hairline to crown and nape. Use soft circular fingertip pressure to relax scalp tension and distribute nutrients evenly around hair roots.
  4. Coat Lengths & Ends: Lightly smooth leftover oil along mid-lengths and tips to guard against wash-day dryness and breakage.
  5. Leave On & Cleanse: Leave for 30–60 minutes before bathing (or overnight on a towel-draped pillow if you have very dry or frizzy hair), then rinse with a gentle cleanser and lukewarm water.

Recommended Weekly Frequency by Hair & Scalp Type

  • Normal to Dry Hair / Frizzy Ends: 2–3 times per week (60 minutes pre-wash or overnight).
  • Oily Scalp: 1–2 times per week as a 30–45 minute pre-wash treatment before shampooing.
  • Curly, Wavy & Coily Indian Hair: 2–3 times weekly pre-wash to preserve curl moisture and reduce detangling breakage.
  • Men & Short Hair: 3–4 drops massaged into scalp 2–3 times weekly before morning or evening shower.

Note: Always perform a small inner-forearm or behind-the-ear patch test before first use. Because Jayakesh uses pure natural ingredients with ZERO chemicals, individual results may vary with diet, water quality, and consistency.
