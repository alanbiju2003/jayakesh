Adapting Your Hair Oiling Routine Across Indian Seasons

How to adjust oil quantity and wash timing across scorching Indian summers, humid monsoons, and cool dry winters with Jayakesh Kerala Hair Oil.

Buy Jayakesh Hair Oil — ₹399 Order on WhatsApp

From coastal Kerala monsoons to dry North Indian winters, seasonal shifts change what your scalp needs:

  • Summer (March–June): Sun and sweat dehydrate strands. Use a light 30–45 minute pre-wash massage twice weekly; Aloe Vera, Hibiscus, and Tulsi soothe sun-parched scalps.
  • Monsoon (June–September): High humidity increases frizz and shedding. Keep scalp clean by applying 5 ml of Jayakesh Hair Oil 40 minutes before every wash rather than leaving heavy oil on unwashed damp hair.
  • Winter (November–February): Cool air dries the scalp; pure unrefined Coconut Oil may thicken naturally below 24°C (a sign of zero paraffin!). Warm the closed bottle briefly in lukewarm water or between your palms and massage 2–3 times weekly.
