Why Red Onion Works Synergistically in Coconut Oil

  • Natural Sulphur Support: Hair shafts are built from keratin proteins cross-linked by disulphide bonds; red onions naturally supply dietary sulphur compounds alongside protective quercetin antioxidants.
  • Deep Carrier Delivery: Pure Coconut Oil's lauric-acid structure penetrates hair fibres effectively while keeping the scalp comfortable and hydrated.
  • Balanced Botanical Harmony: Pairing Red Onion with fresh Tulsi, Hibiscus, and Tea Leaves creates a well-rounded traditional infusion without synthetic perfumes.
